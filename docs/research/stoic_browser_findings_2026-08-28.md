# Stoic browser findings — 28 Aug 2026

## Sources reviewed
- Official features: https://www.getstoic.com/features
- Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.stoicroutine.stoic&hl=en_US

## Observations
- Official site positions Stoic as more than a journal: stoicism/philosophy, mental well-being, happiness, productivity, relationships and practical living.
- Feature pillars: daily reflections, daily prompts, weekly themes, guided journals, progress tracking, streaks/badges, gentle notifications, journaling history, personalized insights, treasured memories, meditation, breathing, writing goals, quotes/affirmations, collections/resources, themed events, privacy/security.
- Google Play listing shows 4.8 stars, 3.24K reviews, 100K+ downloads and in-app purchases at time of review.
- Store copy describes morning preparation + evening reflection, mood tracking, bite-sized exercises, guided journaling, prompts, voice notes, photos/videos, topics including productivity, happiness, gratitude, stress/anxiety, relationships, therapy and self-discovery.
- Additional advertised tools: meditation, breathing, AI mentors (10), sleep content, quotes/affirmations, therapy notes, prompted journal, password lock, streaks/badges, Journey, Trends and export.
- Public store copy contains claims such as “Join over 3 million stoics” while the Google Play install count is 100K+; these should be treated as vendor claims and not automatically reconciled.
- Competitive implication: Stoic sells a broad “mental health companion + journal” promise, while Mentalix currently has a narrower action-first loop and should not copy the entire catalog without evidence.
